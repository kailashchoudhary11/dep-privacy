Defence Exam Prep

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how the Defence Exam Prep app ("the app") handles your information. The app is published by Kailash Choudhary ("we", "us").

Summary

  • You do not need an account or login to use the app.
  • Your practice history stays on your device. We do not collect it.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google collects some information about your device, as described below.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not receive any information that identifies you.

Information stored on your device

To show your progress, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your answers to practice questions and mock tests
  • Your scores, test times and test history
  • Questions you have bookmarked
  • How many mock tests you have taken and unlocked each day

This information never leaves your device through the app. We cannot see it, and we do not receive a copy of it.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads, full-screen ads between tests, and optional video ads that you can choose to watch to unlock an extra mock test. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects the following and sends it to Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Information about your device and the app, such as device model, operating system and app version
  • Your interactions with ads, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data

Google uses this information under its own policies. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads may be personalised based on this information. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (on most phones: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the app under About → Ad privacy options.

The app limits ads to content suitable for teenagers and adults.

Device backup

Android can back up app data to your Google account using its built-in backup feature, if you have backup turned on in your device settings. If so, your progress in this app may be included in that backup and restored when you reinstall the app or set up a new device. Google handles these backups under its own privacy policy. We cannot access them. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or photos. The app has no account system and sends your practice data nowhere.

Deleting your data

You can delete your progress at any time:

  • In the app, open the Progress tab and choose Reset progress from the menu, or
  • Clear the app's storage in your device settings, or
  • Uninstall the app.

Information collected by Google for advertising is held by Google, not by us. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings, and manage your data with Google as described in Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app is intended for people preparing for defence entrance examinations. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before that version is released.
