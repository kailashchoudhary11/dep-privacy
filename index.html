Defence Exam Prep

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

This policy explains how the Defence Exam Prep app ("the app") handles your information. The app is published by Kailash Choudhary ("we", "us").

Summary

  • The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.
  • You do not need an account or login to use the app.
  • Your practice history stays on your device.
  • The app does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

To show your progress, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your answers to practice questions and mock tests
  • Your scores, test times and test history
  • Questions you have bookmarked

This information never leaves your device through the app. We cannot see it, and we do not receive a copy of it.

Device backup

Android can back up app data to your Google account using its built-in backup feature, if you have backup turned on in your device settings. If so, your progress in this app may be included in that backup and restored when you reinstall the app or set up a new device. Google handles these backups under its own privacy policy. We cannot access them. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information. It contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising, and it does not request any device permissions.

Deleting your data

You can delete your progress at any time:

  • In the app, open the Progress tab and choose Reset progress from the menu, or
  • Clear the app's storage in your device settings, or
  • Uninstall the app.

Children

The app is intended for people preparing for defence entrance examinations. It is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, for example by adding advertising, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before that version is released.
